## About Porter Ranch Reserve

Porter Ranch Reserve represents our vision for thoughtful development in the Kalispell area. This exclusive 8-lot subdivision offers the perfect balance of privacy and convenience, with each 5-acre parcel providing ample space for custom home construction while maintaining the natural beauty of the Montana landscape.

**What Makes Porter Ranch Reserve Special:**
- **Privacy & Space**: 5-acre lots ensure your home has room to breathe
- **Custom Builds Only**: Each home is individually designed and built by Swisshaus
- **Agricultural Setting**: Located in historic hay fields with the Blasdel Waterfowl Protection Area to the south
- **Modern Infrastructure**: New road construction with utilities in place
- **Mountain Views**: Stunning vistas of the surrounding Flathead Valley

**Current Availability:**
- 6 lots available for custom home construction
- 1 lot sold
- 1 lot currently under construction ([View Porter Ranch 7](/posts/pr7))
