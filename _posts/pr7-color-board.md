## Starting Notes:
Be aware, with pictures it is very hard to capture the true color of these selections, many of the stock websites don't seem to reflect the actual color of the sample. I do my best to take a photo, and adjust to match the experienced color. Also, there will be some different lighting and times of day photographed due to the shifting nature of color through the day. This is also dependent on your computer monitor coloring. I am using a Macbook as they seem to have very accurate color monitors. 

Secondly, these are colors and choices I had roughly in mind when designing the home. They are not set in stone. If there are selections you do not like, please let me know. I am presenting the whole color scheme because it is important to see the house colors as a whole, but many of these selections don't have to be made right now. Many of these you will be able to see in person. To not hold the job up, and change the scheduling, we need to select the **Interior Door Stain & Window Sill (Same supplier mill and stain)** and the **Cabinet Colors**.  

I think the Door stain will go with many color schemes, so it will be a safe bet. The cabinets are a harder selection, and will involve more personal preference. 

## Design Name

The design in mind for Porter Ranch 7 is a **Montana Ranch Modern** 

Clean and calm white colors contrasted by dark blacks and rich natural materials. 

---

## Base Colors

The two largest selections by amount are the paint and flooring selections. 

**Interior Paint**

For interior paint color, we have really like [Dover White](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-family/white-paint-colors/SW6385-dover-white). We have used this on multiple homes now, and it gives a nice white interior with a hint of warmth to contrast the cold winter days. Hardly noticable, but we have been pleased with it. (*The Sherwin Williams website shows much more color than is actually experienced*)

![Dover White](/assets/blog/pr7/color-board/pr7-CB-Dover-White.jpg)

*This home in Whitefish is painted in Dover White*
![Alpine Meadow House with Interior Color Dover White](/assets/blog/Lidstrom/alpine4.png)
*Slightly Warmer color for the long winter months*
![Slightly Warmer color for the long winter months](/assets/blog/Lidstrom/alpine6.png)

**Flooring**

There are two directions the home could go as we originally envisioned. **Option A:** [Cordalera Merwin](https://cordalerafloors.com/enginereed-hardwood/lakes-collection/merwin/) Rich but not overwhelming walnut color, more of a traditional Montana coloring. **Option B:** [Cordalera Glacier](https://cordalerafloors.com/enginereed-hardwood/lakes-collection/glacier/) Lighter floor color, a brighter home with a Nordic style. 

*These are two options we like, high quality engineered wide plank floor, with a thick wear layer that could be refinished years down the road multiple times.*

## Cabinet Colors

Then we start adding in Cabinet samples.

![Kitchen Cabinet Colors](/assets/blog/pr7/color-board/pr7-CB-Walnut-White.jpg)

This is a sample of **Walnut wood** stained with **'Honey'**.
One of the most expensive wood categories for wood, but a beautiful cabinet. 

Then below the Walnut sample, a sample of **Designer White**.
White will have a nice contrast with the walnut, bright and clean. 

The thought would be that we would use the walnut wood for the Kitchen island, and the master bath cabinets.

Then, the Upper cabinets and wall cabinets in the kitchen would be the white. 

The dark island cabinets in **Walnut-Honey** would contrast well with a white quartz countertop, with a slight warm tone. 

![White Quartz](/assets/blog/pr7/color-board/pr7-CB-White-Quartz.jpg)

![Adding in white quartz for island](/assets/blog/pr7/color-board/pr7-CB-Walnut-White-Quartz.jpg)

But we probably need to use a contrasting countertop color when using the white lower wall cabinets. So a black quartz with not too much movement is added.

![Black Quartz](/assets/blog/pr7/color-board/pr7-CB-Black-Quartz.jpg)
![Added black quartz](/assets/blog/pr7/color-board/pr7-CB-Walnut-White-Quartz-BW.jpg)

To keep on the same color scheme trajectory, we can now think about the master bathroom. The Walnut-Honey would look good with the white quartz, but we have to add in the floor tile. A nice warm white with a good feeling texture, very square edges, 12"x24".

![Add in the bathroom floor tile](/assets/blog/pr7/color-board/pr7-CB-Tile.jpg)

So, let's call this **OPTION A**

Here is another angle for the idea of the texture and sheen on these selections.
